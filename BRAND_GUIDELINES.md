## Decision

Use **Color Lives in the Story** as the one approved visual direction for VidyaMotion. Earlier alternatives, including Quiet Stage and The Alternating Gallery, are rejected and should not be used for new screens or implementation. Older Stitch drafts remain in the project history; the approved homepage is the latest screen, *VidyaMotion — Motion Storyboard Hero*.

[Open the approved homepage in Stitch](https://stitch.withgoogle.com/projects/1941112818307602698)

The homepage borrows the restraint and product-first hierarchy of the [Apple India homepage](https://www.apple.com/in/) as a broad reference only. Do not reproduce Apple branding, copy, typeface, assets, or exact layouts.

## Brand qualities

- **Clear:** Visitors understand the path from an idea to a reviewed storyboard to a finished explainer.
- **Crafted:** Motion, diagrams, captions, and voice are composed to explain the topic.
- **Confident:** Short copy, strong hierarchy, and a small number of deliberate actions.
- **Creative:** Educational visuals carry color and movement; the surrounding product interface stays calm.
- **India-aware:** Use familiar Indian educational examples where relevant, without stereotyped decoration.

Avoid generic AI imagery, robot heads, sparkles, gradient blobs, glassmorphism, dense controls, fake metrics, and invented product capabilities.

## Color tokens

| Role | Color | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Warm white canvas | `#FAF9F6` | Main page background and quiet surfaces |
| Deep ink | `#17191F` | Headlines and primary text |
| Secondary gray | `#666B73` | Supporting text |
| Main action blue | `#315CEB` | Primary buttons, links, and active controls |
| Vermilion | `#F25F4C` | Occasional emphasis in explainer artwork |
| Marigold | `#E9AE45` | Occasional emphasis in explainer artwork |
| Fine divider | `#E8E5DF` | Subtle borders and separators |

Keep the shell mostly warm white, ink, and gray. Reserve vermilion and marigold primarily for the educational animation. Maintain accessible contrast for text and controls; do not communicate status by color alone.

## Typography and layout

- Display: **Manrope**. Interface and body: **Inter**.
- Desktop hero headline: approximately 56–72 px, at most two lines. Section headings: 34–44 px. Body copy: 17–19 px.
- Sentence case, direct language, generous whitespace, an 8 px spacing rhythm, and a content width around 1200 px.
- Use 10–16 px corner radii sparingly. Avoid turning every section into a rounded card.
- Primary action uses solid blue. Secondary actions use text or a restrained outline.

## Homepage direction

The approved Stitch screen is a desktop marketing homepage, not the signed-in creator workspace. Its composition is:

1. **Compact navigation:** VidyaMotion wordmark, How it works, Examples, Sign in, and a clear “Create an explainer” action.
2. **Hero:** “Turn a topic into a clear explainer.” Supporting copy describes a 60–90 second animated explainer with synchronized voiceover and burned-in captions, ready to export or share. The main actions are “Create an explainer” and “Watch the example.”
3. **Focal visual:** A large educational storyboard / video-stage concept. The binary-search example uses three mathematically consistent scenes: initial midpoint 42, narrow to 56/73/88, then find target 73. Captions explain each transition. Blue, marigold, and vermilion distinguish the motion path and key values.
4. **Three-step product story:** Enter or brainstorm a topic; review the combined script and storyboard with conversational revisions; generate, preview, and publish/export.
5. **Confirmed V1 details:** 60–90 second target, vertical 9:16 default with square and landscape alternatives, burned-in captions, MP4 export, Instagram publishing, and Indian-English voice examples Viraj (“Confident and natural”) and Monica (“Interactive and modern”).
6. **Educational examples and final action:** Binary Search, Photosynthesis, and UPI Basics, followed by a restrained “Create an explainer” call to action and minimal footer.

Keep the page focused on the agreed V1. Do not add pricing, usage billing, team features, scheduled publishing, unsupported output specifications, or other social platforms.

## Motion and prototype note

The hero illustration is a **static storyboard concept** depicting a motion sequence. The current Stitch screen does not confirm that the sequence is animated or that its player works. If Stitch prototype interactions are added later, treat them as a click-through demonstration unless actual playback is implemented and verified. Preserve readable labels and caption-safe placement in any real animation.
